# Gold Wanted content and localisation

## Start with the local business facts

Use **Gold Wanted** as the business name. Lead with ink cartridges, refills and toner for Fish Hoek and the South Peninsula. The jewellery-buying service is secondary. Refer to Linda and Brendan as the local team where it helps a visitor.

Keep these statements accurate:

1. Confirmed ink coverage: Canon, HP, Brother, Lexmark and Samsung.
2. Ask customers to send a cartridge-label or printer-model photo and to ask about availability.
3. Gold Wanted buys gold and silver jewellery and selected household silver. Do not say it accepts Kruger Rands, coins or bullion.
4. Sellers need an ID or passport. Stones are returned where possible.
5. Use 082 372 7597 and Shop 5, Triangle Square, 5 De Waal Rd, Fish Hoek, behind Shoprite.

## Write in the approved voice

Use UK/SA spelling, including **jewellery**. Write short, concrete sentences in clear ASD-STE100-style English with natural rhythm. Remove generic AI phrases, unsupported superiority claims, vague stock promises and invented SEO metrics. Do not create customer reviews, ratings or testimonials.

## Maintain local search and social content

1. Give every public page and guide a distinct, natural local primary keyword. Do not stuff keywords or invent volume or difficulty figures.
2. Use the existing local geography only where relevant: Fish Hoek, the South Peninsula, The Valley and nearby suburbs.
3. Preserve title, description, heading, canonical, Open Graph and sitemap consistency when a page changes.
4. Reuse the matching guide image for its article, Facebook adaptation and Google Business Profile post. Do not make a single image stand for an unrelated article.
5. Use honest Google listing links. Do not quote or manufacture reviews.

## Translate and publish safely

English is the default. Use the approved Afrikaans routes only for complete reviewed content. Use **inkpatroon** for an ink cartridge. Keep the language switcher, WhatsApp prompt, page metadata, canonical URL, `hreflang` and audio labels in the matching language.

Do not publish isiXhosa, isiZulu or Sesotho copy or audio until an independent native-speaker review is complete. Do not translate from memory when the approved source copy differs.

## Finish the change

Update affected route links, sitemap and `hreflang`. Check the English and Afrikaans destination pages. Commit to a review branch and wait for Lily’s explicit production approval, unless Lily has explicitly assigned a different authority.
