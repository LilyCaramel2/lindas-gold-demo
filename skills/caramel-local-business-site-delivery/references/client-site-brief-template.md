# Client site brief template

Complete this reference before a substantial site build or refocus. Keep client-specific facts here or in the repository `AGENTS.md`; do not embed them in the reusable skill.

| Area | Record before work starts |
|---|---|
| Client identity | Legal/display business name, owner, operator and agency contact. **Lily Godden is the default approval and release authority**; record a different authority only when Lily explicitly appoints one. |
| Priority services | Services in display order, confirmed brands, availability rules, prices or booking constraints and excluded services. |
| Local conversion | Address, service radius, phone, WhatsApp, email, opening hours, map, Google Business Profile and approved review route. |
| Brand and assets | Complete `brand-asset-intake-template.md` first. Record final logo pack, palette, typography, themes, visual references, image plan, protected assets, prohibited treatments and accessibility constraints. |
| People and photos | Who is shown in supplied images, what must remain visible, which assets are protected and what may be edited. |
| Content | Existing pages, required pages, Journal topics, approved claims, required disclosures, writing standard, spelling and prohibited phrases. |
| Languages | Default language, approved translated routes, native reviewers, terminology, audio requirements and languages held back. |
| Search and social | Distinct local-page focus, verified evidence sources, social channels, Google Business Profile, UTM convention and content planner. |
| Measurement and compliance | Privacy route, cookie choice, GA4 Measurement ID, schema facts, accessibility needs and form-data handling. |
| Delivery | Stack constraint, repository, review branch, preview route, approval gate, production branch and rollback method. |

Record unknown values as **awaiting client input**. Do not fill a field by guessing.
