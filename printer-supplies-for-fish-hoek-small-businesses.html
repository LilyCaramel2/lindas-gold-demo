Printer supplies for Fish Hoek home offices and small businesses.

A printer may be used for invoices, labels, quotations, menus, booking papers and forms. It is easy to forget about ink or toner until a job has to go out.

Notice the early signs

Faded black text, missing colours, lines on the page or a warning message can show that a cartridge or toner is running low. Check the supply before the printer stops completely.

You do not need a large office system to be prepared. Keep the printer model and cartridge code where the person who orders supplies can find them.

Keep the right details on your phone

Take a clear photo of the printer model and the cartridge or toner label. Keep the photos in a shared work chat or on the phone used for ordering. This makes a quick check easier when someone is busy.

It also helps to say what you print. A shop may need labels. A guesthouse may need booking papers. A home office may need invoices and statements. The printer and its use matter.
