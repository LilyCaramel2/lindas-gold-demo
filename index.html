How selling your gold works

Whether you’re clearing out a jewellery box or dealing with an estate, the steps stay simple and transparent.

  1. 1. Bring your gold in

    Chains, rings, bangles, broken pieces, coins – bring what you have. If you’re unsure, Linda will help you sort.

  2. 2. Testing & weighing in front of you

    Each piece is tested and weighed on calibrated scales, right in front of you, with the karat explained in plain language.

  3. 3. Straightforward cash offer

    Linda explains how she arrives at the offer so you understand the numbers. You decide whether to accept.

  4. 4. You’re paid on the spot

    If you accept, you are paid immediately. If not, you simply take your pieces home again.
