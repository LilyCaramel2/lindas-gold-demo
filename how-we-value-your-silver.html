Household silver

Sell silver cutlery in Fish Hoek: what to bring.

Gold Wanted can discuss selected silver jewellery, cutlery, trays and tea services in person at Triangle Square.

Bring the pieces you would like to discuss.

Household silver can include silver jewellery, cutlery, serving pieces, trays and tea services. Bring the items with your ID or passport. A clear conversation starts with the pieces in front of the team.

Coins, Kruger Rands, bullion bars and investment items sit outside this service. Gold Wanted focuses on selected household silver and jewellery.

Look for hallmarks and useful details.

Hallmarks can appear on jewellery clasps, the back of a pendant, the underside of a tray or the handle of cutlery. A mark such as 925 can help identify sterling silver. Keep any useful information with the item when you visit.

Older pieces can carry wear, repairs or mixed materials. The team will look at the item type, the silver content and the practical details that affect the assessment.

Sort family items with care.

Set aside the pieces you want to bring in. Family collections can include jewellery alongside household pieces. A simple tray or box keeps the visit organised.

Gold Wanted can discuss the selected pieces in person. You can also send a WhatsApp message before visiting if you want to describe the items first.

Prepare for your visit.

  • Bring the silver jewellery or household items you want to discuss.
  • Bring your ID or passport.
  • Keep any useful hallmarks or item information with the pieces.
  • Send a WhatsApp message before visiting if you would like to ask a practical question.

Gold Wanted is at Shop 5, Triangle Square, 5 De Waal Rd, Fish Hoek, behind Shoprite.
