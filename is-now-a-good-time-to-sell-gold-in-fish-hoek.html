Gold jewellery guide

Thinking about selling gold jewellery in Fish Hoek?

Gold Wanted offers a local in-person route for people who want to discuss pre-loved and heirloom gold jewellery.

Start with the pieces you want to discuss.

Set aside the gold jewellery you are ready to bring in. Rings, chains, bracelets, pendants and earrings can all form part of the conversation. Keep pieces together in a pouch or small box for your visit.

Gold Wanted works with gold and silver jewellery. Coins, Kruger Rands, bullion bars and investment items sit outside this service.

Bring your ID or passport.

Bring ID or a passport when you visit. This forms part of the in-person selling process. You can send a WhatsApp message before your visit if you would like to ask a practical question about the shop process.

Ask about stones before the assessment.

Stones are returned where possible. Tell the team about any sentimental detail or question you have about a piece. A clear conversation helps you decide which items you would like to discuss.

Choose a local time that works for you.

Gold Wanted is at Shop 5, Triangle Square, behind Shoprite in Fish Hoek. The local setting gives people from Fish Hoek, the Valley and the South Peninsula a nearby place to discuss jewellery in person.
