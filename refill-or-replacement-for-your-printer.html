Cartridge refills in Fish Hoek

When a printer runs out, you need the right information before you decide what to do. A photo of the cartridge or toner gives us a better starting point than the printer brand alone.

Look at the cartridge code

Find the code on the empty cartridge, toner or box. Take a photo of the full label. The code helps identify the item. Two cartridges can look alike but fit different printers.

If the printer uses separate black and colour cartridges, photograph both. If you have the old box, include that in the photo as well.

Ask about a suitable option

You can ask Gold Wanted to check whether a refill or replacement option suits your item. The answer depends on the printer model, the cartridge code and current availability. HP 650 Black, HP 650 Tri-colour, HP 22 Tri-colour and Canon 445/446 each need their own model and label check.

Each cartridge and replacement has its own supply route. A quick check gives you information that applies to your printer.

Bring the empty cartridge if you can

The empty cartridge can help identify the right supply. Bring it with you when it is easy to carry. Send a photo before travelling when you prefer to check first.

This is useful for homes, offices and small businesses in Fish Hoek and the South Peninsula. It gives you a clear answer before you make a trip.
