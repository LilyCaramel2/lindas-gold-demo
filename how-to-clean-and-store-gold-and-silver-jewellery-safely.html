Jewellery care

Clean and store gold jewellery with care.

A few simple habits help keep cherished gold and silver pieces tidy, dry and easy to enjoy.

Use a soft clean cloth.

Wipe jewellery gently with a soft clean cloth after wearing it. This removes everyday surface marks and helps keep the piece ready for its next use.

Use a light touch around clasps, stones, engraving and fine chains. A professional jeweller can help with a piece that needs specialist care.

Keep pieces dry before storage.

Dry jewellery before placing it in a box or pouch. Moisture can affect the surface of many metals and can make storage less tidy over time.

Store chains and small pieces separately.

A lined box, soft pouch or divided tray keeps pieces separate. This helps chains stay easy to use and makes rings, earrings and pendants easier to find.

Make a simple family jewellery routine.

Set aside a small place for everyday pieces. Keep sentimental jewellery in a labelled pouch or box. A short regular check helps you keep track of the pieces you value.
