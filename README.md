# Linda’s Gold – Demo Website

Static demo website for **Linda’s Gold**, a small gold & silver buyer in Fish Hoek, Cape Town.  
Built as a simple multi-page HTML/CSS site to show Linda what her shop could look like online.

## Live demo

_Deployed via Vercel (link to be added once deployed)._

## Pages

- `index.html` – Home  
  Hero, what Linda buys, how it works, cartridges teaser, and a quick contact form.

- `about.html` – About  
  Short story about Linda, how she works, and what she buys.

- `cartridges.html` – Ink cartridges  
  Small “side hustle” page for local ink and toner sales.

- `blog.html` – Blog / notebook  
  A few demo article cards with topics about selling gold and making ink last longer.

- `contact.html` – Contact  
  Address, hours, contact details, and a simple enquiry form.

All pages share a single stylesheet: `lindas-gold.css`.

## Tech stack

- Plain **HTML5**  
- Custom **CSS** (no frameworks)  
- Hosted on **GitHub**  
- Deployed to **Vercel** as a static site
