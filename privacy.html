Privacy & Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Linda's Gold collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you interact with our website or visit our studio in Fish Hoek.

Data Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us through our contact forms, WhatsApp inquiries, or during in-person valuations at our studio. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and details regarding the items you wish to have valued.

We only collect data that is necessary for providing our services, processing valuations, and communicating with you regarding your inquiries.

Cookie Usage

Our website uses essential cookies to ensure basic functionality and to improve your browsing experience. These include:

  • • Essential Cookies: Required for the site to function securely.
  • • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings, such as cookie consent choices.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or via our on-site cookie banner.

User Rights

Under South African data protection laws (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • • Request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • • Request the deletion of your personal information.
  • • Object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.

Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All valuation records are kept confidential and stored securely in accordance with professional standards.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at our Fish Hoek studio.
