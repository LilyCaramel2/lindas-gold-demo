Privacy and cookies

How Gold Wanted handles your information.

Updated 15 August 2026. This page explains the information connected to website enquiries, WhatsApp messages and shop visits.

Who handles your information?

Gold Wanted operates from Shop 5, Triangle Square, 5 De Waal Rd, Fish Hoek. Linda and Brendan use customer information to respond to enquiries, check printer-supply details and manage in-person service conversations.

Information connected to an enquiry.

When you contact Gold Wanted through WhatsApp, email or a website form, your message may include your name, phone number, email address, printer details or details about items you want to discuss. Gold Wanted uses this information to respond to you and manage the related service.

Cookies and appearance preferences.

The website uses a small preference record to remember your cookie choice and screen appearance. Essential site functions use this record. Google Analytics begins only after you choose Allow analytics.

You can change the website cookie choice at any time by clearing site data in your browser. You can also contact Gold Wanted on WhatsApp if you have a question about information connected to your enquiry.

Third-party services.

WhatsApp processes messages through its own service. Google Maps, Google Business Profile and Facebook may handle information when you choose to use those services. Google Analytics processes limited website usage and conversion activity only when you choose Allow analytics. Their own privacy information applies to the activity that takes place on their platforms.

Your questions and requests.

You can ask about personal information connected to a Gold Wanted enquiry. Send a WhatsApp message to 082 372 7597 or visit the shop at Triangle Square, Fish Hoek. Gold Wanted will respond through the contact details linked to the enquiry.

Gold Wanted reviews this page when website services or data handling changes.
