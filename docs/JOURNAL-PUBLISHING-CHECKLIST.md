# Gold Wanted Journal — Monthly Publishing Checklist

Use this checklist when adding a genuine local article or shop note to `blog.html`. The Journal is a public resource for Fish Hoek printer-supply information and approved gold/silver-jewellery guidance; it is never a place for invented offers, stock claims or location-only pages.

## Before drafting

1. Confirm the real customer question, service/category and local relevance with Linda or Brendan.
2. Select one conservative phrase: **Fish Hoek + actual service/category + customer problem or use case**.
3. Confirm any stock, price, hours, product, collection or promotion statement directly with the owner.
4. Decide whether the page is English-only or has a fully approved Afrikaans equivalent. Add `hreflang` only when both full versions exist.

## Article package

1. Write a useful 800–900-word article with a specific title, one local purpose and a practical WhatsApp/location call to action.
2. Prepare one approved local or product image in WebP for the article card and one suitable social/Google Business Profile crop.
3. Create a distinct Facebook and Google Business Profile adaptation; use only verified profiles and truthful offer terms.
4. Add an audio script only after the written article receives content-policy review.

## Static-site update

1. Create a clear, stable filename in the site root, for example `check-ink-availability-in-fish-hoek.html`.
2. Add a canonical URL, title, meta description, meaningful image alt text and one relevant internal link.
3. Add the article card to `blog.html` under the appropriate Ink & Toner or Jewellery group.
4. Add the published URL to `sitemap.xml` and validate the new page, card link, images and light/dark appearance on phone and desktop.
5. After release, inspect the URL in Search Console and record the real published URL in the monthly content log.

## Guardrails

- Keep Ink/Toner and Gold/Silver service prominence balanced across the overall site.
- Refer to the actual Fish Hoek counter and confirmed service area; avoid generic suburb-page expansion.
- Keep WhatsApp as the primary enquiry action until the owner approves a different route.
- Do not activate HubSpot or email marketing merely because content or form fields are ready.
