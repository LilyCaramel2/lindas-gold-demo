About Linda’s Gold

Linda’s Gold is a small, owner-run gold and silver buying shop in Fish Hoek. You deal directly with Linda — no call centres, no fuss.

She takes the time to explain what you have, how it’s tested, and how your offer is worked out. If you decide not to sell, you simply take your items home again. No pressure, ever.

Her focus is on fair, straightforward offers, instant payment and a calm, private space where you can ask questions without feeling rushed or embarrassed.

What Linda buys

• Gold jewellery – even broken chains and single earrings
• Silver jewellery and silverware
• Coins and Krugerrands
• No loose stones or uncertified diamonds
